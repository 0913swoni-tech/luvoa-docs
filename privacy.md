# **LUVOA Privacy Policy**

**Last Updated: June 28, 2026**

1. Introduction
Prime Node ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") operates the LUVOA application. We believe that privacy is the ultimate luxury. This Privacy Policy outlines our strict guidelines regarding the collection, use, and absolute protection of your information when you use our application.

2. Absolute On-Device Principle & Zero-Logging
LUVOA is meticulously engineered on a "Local-First Hybrid Architecture."

Core Data Isolation: Your personal settings, favorite quotes, and usage history are stored entirely and exclusively on your local device.

Zero-Logging: We do not transmit, collect, or store your personal usage data, reading history, or quotes on our servers. You have absolute ownership and control over your data.

3. Data Deletion and User Responsibility
Because your data is stored locally on your device to ensure maximum privacy, deleting the LUVOA app will permanently and irreversibly erase all your saved data. We do not retain backup copies on our servers, and we cannot recover data lost due to device upgrades, loss, damage, or app uninstallation. Managing device backups (such as iCloud or Google Drive backups) is solely the user's responsibility.

4. Limited Data Collection for Service Stability
To provide a seamless 0ms boot experience and ensure application stability, we may collect strictly anonymized and non-identifiable data:

Usage Data: Anonymous diagnostic data (e.g., app crash logs) used exclusively to improve app stability.

Device Information: Non-identifiable device tokens required solely to deliver silent push notifications (e.g., Daily Quotes).

5. Third-Party Infrastructure & Payment Security
To provide VVIP membership features and maintain core infrastructure, we utilize trusted, world-class third-party services. These services are bound by strict confidentiality agreements:

Apple App Store & Google Play Services: For app distribution and foundational services.

RevenueCat: Utilized to securely process in-app purchases and manage premium subscriptions. We do not collect, process, or store your credit card details or financial information. All billing is handled directly by Apple or Google via RevenueCat's secure infrastructure.

Firebase (Google): Utilized exclusively for Cloud Messaging and delivering push notifications.

Supabase: Utilized for secure, anonymized data synchronization and core backend infrastructure, employing Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure absolute data isolation.

6. Protection of Minors (Children's Privacy)
LUVOA is designed as a high-end application for mature audiences. We strictly restrict the use of our service by individuals under the age of 14. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 14. If we become aware that a user under 14 has provided us with personal data outside of the on-device environment, we will take immediate steps to permanently delete such information from our systems.

7. Data Security
We employ commercially acceptable, industry-standard security measures, including Row Level Security (RLS) and encryption, to protect any anonymized data handled by our infrastructure. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
