# **LUVOA Privacy Policy**

**Last Updated: June 28, 2026**

**1. Introduction**
Prime Node ("we," "us," or "our") operates the LUVOA application. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app and the choices you have associated with that data.

**2. Local-First Architecture & Data Collection**
LUVOA is built on a "Local-First Hybrid Architecture." This means that your primary data, including your interactions with quotes and personal settings, is stored locally on your device. We do not harvest or store your private usage data on our servers.
We may collect limited data strictly necessary to provide our core services:

- **Usage Data:** Anonymous diagnostic data (such as app crash logs) to improve app stability and ensure a seamless boot experience.
- **Device Information:** Non-identifiable device tokens required to deliver silent push notifications (e.g., daily quote triggers).

**3. Third-Party Services**
We employ trusted third-party companies and individuals to facilitate our service, provide the service on our behalf, or assist us in analyzing how our service is used. These third parties have access to your data only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose:

- **Apple & Google Play Services:** For app distribution and foundational services.
- **RevenueCat:** For securely processing in-app purchases and managing premium subscriptions. We do not directly collect or store your payment card details.
- **Firebase (Google):** For delivering essential cloud messaging and push notifications.
- **Supabase:** For secure, anonymized data synchronization and core infrastructure support.

**4. Security of Data**
The security of your data is important to us. By utilizing Row Level Security (RLS) and industry-standard encryption, we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

**5. Children's Privacy**
Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from children without verification of parental consent, we take steps to remove that information from our servers immediately.

**6. Changes to This Privacy Policy**
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

**7. Contact Us**
